### AACP-A-01 Use-limited authorization survives a persistence boundary (A)

- Specification: SPECIFICATION.md §2 A: question 'verified as authentic, unmodified and currently valid?'; in scope 'signatures or MACs, tamper detection, expiry, not-before, maximum age, replay semantics, single-use semantics, and binding to an identity or proposal'
- Rationale: A use limit carried by the authorization artifact is part of what makes it currently valid. For N = 1 the limit is single-use semantics, named verbatim in A's scope; an artifact accepted on use N + 1 is not currently valid.
- Boundary: Applies only when the limit is part of the authorization artifact itself (for example a signed caveat). Quotas or rate limits enforced outside the artifact are not A; rate limiting is outside the model (SPECIFICATION §10).

### AACP-B-01 Unauthenticated principal accepted (B)

- Specification: SPECIFICATION.md §2 B: question 'who or what held authority to authorize the action?'; in scope 'actor identity, policy authority, reviewer or approver role enforcement, delegation chains and attenuation, tenant scope, and provenance of approved intent'
- Rationale: Actor identity is in B's scope. If the actor is taken from an unauthenticated field, the system cannot establish who held authority.
- Boundary: Not a failure if the actor is established by an authenticated channel on the mandatory path (for example mTLS identity mapped by the enforcement point); the probe must show the request is allowed with the actor field alone.

### AACP-B-02 Principal key is self-attested (B)

- Specification: SPECIFICATION.md §2 B: question 'who or what held authority to authorize the action?'; in scope 'actor identity, policy authority, reviewer or approver role enforcement, delegation chains and attenuation, tenant scope, and provenance of approved intent'
- Rationale: A key supplied by the request is bound to the request, not to the principal. Without a verifier-held principal-to-key binding, a valid signature does not establish who held authority.
- Boundary: Not a failure when the request-supplied key is itself certified to the principal by a trusted issuer that the verifier checks.

### AACP-B-03 Bearer authorization not bound to a subject (B)

- Specification: SPECIFICATION.md §2 B: question 'who or what held authority to authorize the action?'; in scope 'actor identity, policy authority, reviewer or approver role enforcement, delegation chains and attenuation, tenant scope, and provenance of approved intent'
- Rationale: Provenance of approved intent includes to whom authority was granted. An artifact usable by any requester carries no record of the principal it was issued to.
- Boundary: A deliberately bearer design is a claim boundary. If the implementer states bearer semantics as a non-claim, the row is OUT_OF_SCOPE for that path, not FAIL.

### AACP-C-01 Authorization not bound to the exact call (C)

- Specification: SPECIFICATION.md §2 C: question 'Is the authorization bound to the exact call that is executed?'; in scope 'tool identity, full argument binding, target, tenant, proposal identity, call identity, immutable binding such as a canonical hash recomputed at dispatch, and resistance to argument substitution under a replayed or reused call identity'
